Eminent domain study.
Sponsored by: Representative(s) Winter
A BILL
for
1 AN ACT relating to eminent domain; requiring the joint
2 corporations, elections and political subdivisions interim
3 committee to commence a study regarding eminent domain as
4 specified; requiring a report; and providing for an
5 effective date.
6
7 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Wyoming:
8
9 Section 1.
10
11 (a) During the 2025 interim, the joint corporations,
12 elections and political subdivisions interim committee
13 shall study issues surrounding the use of the power of
14 eminent domain in the state. As part of the study, the
15 committee shall consider the following issues at a minimum:

6
7 (iii) Liability for damages caused by the
8 utilization of property acquired through eminent domain;
9
10 (iv) Reasons the power of eminent domain can be
11 used;
12
13 (v) Parties authorized to use the power of

15
