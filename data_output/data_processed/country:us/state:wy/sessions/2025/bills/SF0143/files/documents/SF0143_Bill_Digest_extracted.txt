SF0143S2001/FAILED
Page 4-lines 19 After "banks." insert "The state treasurer shall not
deposit public monies in a credit union unless the
credit union is within ten (10) miles of the office of
the treasurer and there is no bank that qualifies as a
depository for public monies within ten (10) miles of
the treasurer's office.".
Page 11-line 6 After "W.S. 9-4-803(a)." insert "The treasurer of a
political subdivision, municipality or special district
shall not deposit public monies in a credit union unless
the credit union is within ten (10) miles of the office
of the treasurer and there is no bank that qualifies as
a depository for public monies within ten (10) miles of
the applicable treasurer's office.". CRUM
