HB0165 Ranked choice voting-prohibition.
Haroldson, Heiner, Johnson, Ottman, Pendergraft,
Smith, S, Webber and Wharff and Senator(s) Hutchings
and Ide
AN ACT relating to elections; clarifying that elections shall not be conducted
by ranked choice voting; clarifying that ballots are required to specify only
one (1) vote per office to be filled; and providing for an effective date.
