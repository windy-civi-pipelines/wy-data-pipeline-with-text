Section 1:
FISCAL NOTE
FY 2026 FY 2027 FY 2028
NON-ADMINISTRATIVE IMPACT
Anticipated Revenue increase
GAME & FISH FUND $686,230 $686,230 $686,230
Source of revenue increase:
This bill increases annual nonresident fishing licenses from $100 to $110, daily
nonresident fishing licenses from $12 to $14, and five-day nonresident fishing
licenses from $54 to $60, effective July 1, 2025.
Assumptions:
The Game and Fish Department assumes the number of nonresident fishing licenses
issued are consistent with the five-year average for calendar years 2019 through
2023, resulting in an estimated annual revenue increase of $686,230 to the Game and
Fish Fund.
The average annual revenue increase to the Game and Fish fund in the form of
licensing fees for each nonresident fishing license is calculated as follows:
• Annual nonresident fishing license: 22,381 licenses x $10 fee increase equates
to an estimated $223,810 annual revenue increase;
• Daily nonresident fishing license: 159,875 licenses x $2 fee increase equates
to an estimated $319,950 annual revenue increase
• Five consecutive day nonresident fishing license: 22,317 licenses x $6 fee
increase equates to an estimated $142,470 annual revenue increase.
