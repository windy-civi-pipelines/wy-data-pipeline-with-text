Section 1:
HB0183 Net metering amendments.
Kelly, Lucas, Pendergraft and Winter and Senator(s)
Boner and Pearson
AN ACT relating to electric utilities; amending the generation capacity for net
metering systems; providing for the aggregation of net metering systems;
authorizing customer-generators with specified net metering systems to charge
a customer-generator fee or increase in a monthly fee; providing and amending
definitions; and providing for an effective date.
