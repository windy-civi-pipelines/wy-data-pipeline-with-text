Section 1:
HB0028 Commercial driver's licenses-revisions.
Interim Committee
AN ACT relating to motor vehicles; amending grounds for disqualification from
driving a commercial motor vehicle for persons under twenty-one (21) years of
age; authorizing expunged records to be considered for purposes of
disqualification from driving a commercial motor vehicle as specified; adding
violations that qualify as serious traffic violations; authorizing the
department to waive examinations for a commercial driver's license applicant as
specified; providing rulemaking authority; and providing for effective dates.
