HB0173 Independent candidate requirements.
Bratten, Brown, G, Filer, Geringer, Hoeft, Johnson,
Knapp, Lien, Locke, McCann, Rodriguez-Williams,
Schmid, Singh, Smith, S, Webb, Webber, Wharff and
Winter and Senator(s) Boner, Pearson, Smith, D and
Steinmetz
AN ACT relating to elections; requiring independent candidates to swear that
they are unaffiliated or not registered with a major or minor political party;
revising the number of signatures required for independent candidates to appear
on the general election ballot; revising the deadline to file a petition as an
independent candidate; and providing for an effective date.
