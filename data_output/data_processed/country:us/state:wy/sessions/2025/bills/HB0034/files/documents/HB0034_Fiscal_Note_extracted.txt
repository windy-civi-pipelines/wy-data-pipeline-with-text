FISCAL NOTE
This bill contains an appropriation of $750,000 from the HIGHWAY FUND to the
Governor's Office. This appropriation is effective immediately.
DETAIL OF APPROPRIATION
Agency #: 001 Agency Name: Governor's Office
Unit: N/A
EXPENDITURE BY SERIES AND YEAR FY 2025 FY 2026 FY 2027
0900 Contractual Services Costs $0 $750,000 $0
Total Expenditure Per Year: $0 $750,000 $0
Grand Total Expenditure: $750,000
Total Appropriated to Agency: $750,000
Total Appropriated by Fund:
HIGHWAY FUND $750,000
Description of appropriation:
The appropriation will be used to fund the efficiency study of the Wyoming Department
of Transportation (Department). The Governor’s Office will hire an independent
third party to present a final report not later than July 1, 2026, containing the
following information:
• An evaluation of the Department’s rules, policies and procedures;
• A review of the Department’s staffing and resources and the distribution and
number of staff and resources around Wyoming;
• A review of the Department’s design and construction practices and
technologies, including stand specifications for engineering;
• An anonymous survey of all Department employees asking for areas of
efficiencies within the Department;
• A determination whether efficiencies or cost savings can be gained;
• Recommendations for the Department to achieve identified efficiencies.
Prepared by: Matthew Willmarth, LSO Phone: 777-7881
(Information provided by Rory Horsley, Governor's Office, 777-5010; Rodney Freier,
Jr., Department of Transportation, 777-4174)
