Section 1:
HB0329 Office of homeland security amendments.
Guggenmos, Lien, Singh, Washut and Yin and Senator(s)
Crago and Smith, D
AN ACT relating to the office of homeland security; making the office of homeland
security's cooperation with homeland security agencies in other states and of
the federal government discretionary; authorizing the office of homeland
security to cooperate with Wyoming law enforcement agencies as specified;
requiring the division of criminal investigation to cooperate with the office
of homeland security; and providing for an effective date.
