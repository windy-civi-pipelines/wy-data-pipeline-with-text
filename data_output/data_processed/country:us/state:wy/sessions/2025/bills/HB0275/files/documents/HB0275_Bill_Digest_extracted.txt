HB0275 Treatment of animals.
and Senator(s) Barlow, Hutchings, Landen, Nethercott

AN ACT relating to crimes and offenses; providing a misdemeanor offense and
amending the offense of felony animal cruelty to address actions where wildlife
is reduced to possession; prohibiting the torture of wildlife as specified;
specifying the required treatment of certain wildlife; specifying penalties;
providing for license revocation and suspension and forfeiture of devices and
equipment for specified convictions; clarifying trapping requirements; removing
a reporting requirement; providing definitions; making conforming amendments;
and providing for an effective date.

Page 5-after line 15 Insert:
"(a) The following shall apply to the taking of wildlife:
(i) When on a person's private land, no person shall harass,
pursue, hunt, shoot, or kill any Wyoming wildlife except predatory animals with,
from, or by use of any aircraft, automotive vehicle, trailer, motor-propelled
wheeled vehicle, or vehicle designed for travel over snow;.
(ii) When on public land, no person shall:
(A) Pursue, hunt, shoot or kill any Wyoming wildlife except
predatory animals or predacious birds from or by use of any aircraft, automotive
vehicle, trailer, motor-propelled wheeled vehicle or vehicle designed for travel
over snow;
(B) Harass, injure or kill any Wyoming wildlife, predatory
animals or predacious birds with an automotive vehicle, trailer, motor-propelled
wheeled vehicle or vehicle designed for travel over snow.
(iii) No person shall use any aircraft, to aid in the taking of
any Wyoming wildlife, except predatory animals, whether by spotting or locating
the wildlife, communicating with any person attempting to take the wildlife, or
by providing other aid to any person taking the wildlife. Nothing in this
subsection shall apply to the use of any aircraft by governmental agencies,
their employees, contractors or designees performing any lawful duties. The
commission may exempt handicapped hunters from any provision of this subsection.
For purposes of this section "aircraft" means any machine or device capable of
atmospheric flight including but not limited to an airplane, helicopter, glider,
dirigible or unmanned aerial vehicle.". SCHMID, PROVENZA
