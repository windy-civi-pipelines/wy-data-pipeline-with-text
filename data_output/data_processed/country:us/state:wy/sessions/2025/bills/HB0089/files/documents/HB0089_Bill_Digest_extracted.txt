Section 1:
Sponsored By: Representative(s) Riggins, Bear, Campbell, K,
Guggenmos and Strock
AN ACT relating to industrial development and siting; requiring decommissioned
wind turbine blades and towers to be disposed of on the site of the wind
turbines; prohibiting the disposal of wind turbine blades and towers in
municipal solid waste facilities; specifying exceptions; making conforming
amendments; requiring rulemaking; specifying applicability; and providing for
effective dates.
