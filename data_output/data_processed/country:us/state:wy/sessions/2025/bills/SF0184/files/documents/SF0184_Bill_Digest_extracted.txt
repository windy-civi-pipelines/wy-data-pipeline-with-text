Section 1:
Sponsored By: Senator(s) Smith, D, Ide and Laursen, D and
Representative(s) Lucas and Smith, S
AN ACT relating to elections; requiring the use of paper ballots for elections;
requiring hand counting of paper ballots; specifying procedures for hand
counting of paper ballots; repealing provisions related to electronic voting
machines and electronic pollbooks; revising procedures for the post-election
ballot audit; providing for testing of hand counting, tabulating and reporting
systems; providing for election observers; providing penalties; making
conforming amendments; providing for rulemaking; and providing for an effective
date.
