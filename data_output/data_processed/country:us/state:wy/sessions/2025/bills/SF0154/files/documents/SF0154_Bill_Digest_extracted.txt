Section 1:
Industrial, wind and solar projects-hearing
SF0154
deadline.
Campbell, K and Lien
AN ACT relating to industrial development and siting; amending the deadline by
which the director of the department of environmental quality must hold a
hearing on industrial siting permit applications; amending deadlines to cure
incomplete applications; amending deadlines for counties to hold hearings and
reach decisions on permit applications for wind and solar projects; specifying
applicability; and providing for an effective date.
