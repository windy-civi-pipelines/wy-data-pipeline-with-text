Section 1:
2025 STATE OF WYOMING 25LSO-0077
SENATE FILE NO. SF0026
Protection of military equipment.
Affairs Interim Committee
A BILL
for
1 AN ACT relating to crimes and offenses; providing an
2 affirmative defense for use of force to protect military
3 equipment; extending immunity for civil or criminal
4 liability for acts committed in the performance of
5 necessary duties to state security officers employed by the
6 military department; providing a definition; specifying
7 applicability; and providing for an effective date.
8
9 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Wyoming:
10
11 Section 1. W.S. 6-2-603 is created to read:
12
13 6-2-603. Use of force in defense of military
14 equipment.
15
1 SF0026
2025 STATE OF WYOMING 25LSO-0077
1 A state security officer as defined by W.S.
2 19-7-101(a)(xiii), a member of the armed forces or a member
3 of the Wyoming national guard in active state service as
4 defined by W.S. 19-7-101(a)(v) who is on official duty
5 defending military equipment is privileged to use
6 reasonable force as necessary to defend military equipment,
7 including deadly force as defined by W.S. 6-2-602(g)(iii),
8 in accordance with published military regulations and
9 doctrine regarding the use of force.
10
11 Section 2. W.S. 19-7-101(a) by creating a new
12 paragraph (xiii) and 19-9-401(c)(intro) are amended to
13 read:
14
15 19-7-101. Definitions.
16
17 (a) As used in this act:
18
19 (xiii) "State security officer" means a person
20 employed by the military department whose primary duties
21 include being armed and securing military assets controlled
22 by the military department.
23
2 SF0026
2025 STATE OF WYOMING 25LSO-0077
1 19-9-401. National Guard members granted certain
2 exemptions.
3
4 (c) No member of the Wyoming national guard in active
5 state service or a state security officer shall incur any
6 personal liability, civil or criminal, by reason of acts
7 committed in the performance of his necessary duties
8 incident to service under orders of the governor or any
9 lawful superior if the acts are not palpably illegal,
10 excessively violent or malicious. If any civil action is
11 brought against a member or state security officer by
12 reason of an act or acts committed in the performance of
13 his necessary duties, he is entitled to legal counsel to
14 assist in his defense at state expense. Legal counsel for
15 such purposes may be chosen by the guardsman or state
16 security officer subject to approval by the Wyoming
17 attorney general and legal fees incurred shall be approved
18 by either:
19
20 Section 3. The affirmative defense created by section
21 1 of this act shall be available as an affirmative defense
22 to conduct occurring on and after July 1, 2025.
23
3 SF0026
2025 STATE OF WYOMING 25LSO-0077
1 Section 4. This act is effective July 1, 2025.
2
3 (END)
