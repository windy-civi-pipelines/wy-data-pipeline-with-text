Section 1:
SF0111 Net metering revisions.
Larsen, L
AN ACT relating to net metering; revising how net metering compensation applies
to new small customer‑generators; authorizing the public service commission to
authorize a different system of compensation for excess energy production if
service is being subsidized by other customers; clarifying how net metering
compensation applies to existing small customer-generators; providing
definitions; making conforming amendments; requiring rulemaking; and providing
for an effective date.
