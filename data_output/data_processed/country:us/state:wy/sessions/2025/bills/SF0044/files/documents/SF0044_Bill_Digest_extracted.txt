Section 1:
Sponsored By: Senator(s) Schuler, Brennan, Crago and Dockstader and
Representative(s) Davis and Harshman
AN ACT relating to education; requiring students at the University of Wyoming
and Wyoming community colleges to compete in intercollegiate athletic
competitions based on their biological sex; providing definitions; and providing
for an effective date.

Page 2-after line 9 Insert and renumber as necessary:
"(iii) "Female" means a person who has, had, will have or would
have had, but for a congenital anomaly or intentional or unintentional
disruption, the reproductive system that at some point produces, transports and
utilizes eggs for fertilization;
(iv) "Male" means a person who has, had, will have or would have
had, but for a congenital anomaly or intentional or unintentional disruption,
the reproductive system that at some point produces, transports and utilizes
sperm for fertilization.".
Page 2-after line 21 Insert and renumber as necessary:
"(iii) Participation in any athletic competition designated for
students of the female sex with or against a team that the educational
institution knows has on the team a student of the male sex;
(iv) Except as provided by subsection (c) of this section, in the
case of an individual sport, students of the female sex to directly compete
against a student that the educational institution knows is a student of the
male sex if the educational institution determines that the competition would
be unsafe or unfair to the students of the female sex.".
Page 3-line 7 Delete ":" and insert ",".
Page 3-lines 9 through 14 Delete entirely.
Page 3-line 16 Delete "(ii)".
Page 4-after line 11 Insert and renumber as necessary:
"(g) A person may seek declaratory and injunctive relief against an
educational institution for any violation of this section. The court may order
injunctive or other equitable relief. The court may award the prevailing party
reasonable attorney's fees and costs.".
Page 4-line 13 Delete "(g)" and insert "(h)". ANDREW, CHAIRMAN
SF0044HW001/ADOPTED (TO ENGROSSED COPY)
Page 2-after line 9 In the House standing committee amendment
(SF0044HS001/ACE) to this line, after paragraph (iv)
inserted by that amendment, insert and renumber as
necessary:
"(v) "Individual sport" means an athletic activity where
participants compete as individuals for a time, score or result, regardless of
whether their individual time, score or result contributes to an overall team
score.". LAWLEY
