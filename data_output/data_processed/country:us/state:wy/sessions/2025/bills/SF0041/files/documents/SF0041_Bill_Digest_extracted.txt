SF0041 Federal acts-legal actions authorized.
Resources Interim Committee
AN ACT relating to the legislature; authorizing the legislature to prosecute
actions involving the proper administration and interpretation of federal acts;
making conforming amendments; authorizing the management council to initiate
litigation upon a majority vote; providing legislative findings; providing for
the use of funds for litigation purposes; and providing for an effective date.
