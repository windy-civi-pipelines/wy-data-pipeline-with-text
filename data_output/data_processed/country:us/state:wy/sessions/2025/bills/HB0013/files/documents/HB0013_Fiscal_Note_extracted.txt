Raw Text:
Integrated test center-governance. 25LSO-0045, 1.0
FISCAL NOTE
This bill transfers all property, implements, fixtures, facilities, equipment,
obligations, rights and unexpended funds of the integrated test center owned or
held by the state of Wyoming to the University of Wyoming School of Energy Resources
on the effective date of this act.
This bill requires the Governor’s Office, Wyoming Energy Authority, and School of
Energy Resources, to the greatest extent possible, take all actions necessary to
transfer any existing agreements or contracts of the integrated test center to the
school of energy resources.
The University of Wyoming indicates this bill provides for the School of Energy
Resources to include a budget for the integrated test center in its budget submitted
to the Governor and if circumstances change and costs to operate the integrated
test center increase, the budget process will be utilized to request necessary
funding.
Prepared by: Matthew Willmarth, LSO Phone: 777-7881
(Information provided by Rory L. Horsley, Governor's Office, 777-5010; Alex Kean,
University of Wyoming, 766-9028; Jami Blosmo, Energy Authority, 777-3521)
