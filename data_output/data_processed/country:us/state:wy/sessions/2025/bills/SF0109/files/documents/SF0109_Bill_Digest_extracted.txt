districts.

AN ACT relating to education; allowing children to enroll in any public school
within their resident school district as specified; providing exceptions;
providing priority for specified students; requiring reporting by school
districts; amending the duties of the school district board of trustees;
requiring the state superintendent of public instruction to publish reported
information; making conforming amendments; and providing for an effective date.
