Section 1:
By the people act. 25LSO-0468, 1.2
FISCAL NOTE
FY 2026 FY 2027 FY 2028
NON-ADMINISTRATIVE IMPACT
Anticipated Expenditure increase
GENERAL FUND $3,600 $0 $0
Source of expenditure increase:
Resources will be for the purchase of video conferencing cameras, such as the
Meeting Owl 3, plus expansion microphones.
Assumptions:
Expenditure increase is based on a current quote for an Owl Labs Meeting Owl 3
with an Expansion Microphone – three owls with expansion microphones at $1,200
per unit assuming shipping is free.
The Department of Administration & Information (DA&I) has indicated that the
estimate of administrative impact submission by the Department of Enterprise
Technology Services will encompass the impact to DA&I.
No significant fiscal or personnel impact to the University of Wyoming.
NOTICE-AGENCY ESTIMATE OF ADMINISTRATIVE IMPACT REQUESTED
This bill has administrative impact that appears to increase duties or
responsibilities of one or more state agencies and may impact agency spending or
staffing requirements. As introduced, the bill does not modify any state agency
budget or current personnel authorizations.
The following state agencies will be asked to provide their estimate of the
administrative fiscal impact prior to the first committee meeting held to
consider the bill:
Enterprise Technology Services
Community College Commission
