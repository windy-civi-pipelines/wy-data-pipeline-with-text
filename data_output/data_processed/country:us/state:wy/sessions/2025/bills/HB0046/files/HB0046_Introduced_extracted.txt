Section 1:
2025 STATE OF WYOMING 25LSO-0409
Homeschool freedom act.
Sponsored by: Representative(s) Strock, Andrew, Angelos,
Campbell, K, Haroldson, Heiner, Neiman,
Ottman and Rodriguez-Williams
A BILL
for
1 AN ACT relating to education; removing the requirement that
2 a home-based educational program submit curriculum to the
3 local school board; removing a provision relating to the
4 failure to submit curriculum; and providing for an
5 effective date.
6
7 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Wyoming:
8
9 Section 1. W.S. 21-4-102(b) is amended to read:
10
11 21-4-102. When attendance required; exemptions;
12 withdrawal.
13
14 (b) A home-based educational program shall meet the
15 requirements of a basic academic educational program
1 HB0046
2025 STATE OF WYOMING 25LSO-0409
1 pursuant to W.S. 21-4-101(a)(vi). It shall be the
2 responsibility of every person administering a home-based
3 educational program to submit ensure a curriculum to the
4 local board of trustees each year showing is administered
5 to pupils in the program that the program complies with the
6 requirements of this subsection. Failure to submit a
7 curriculum showing compliance is prima facie evidence that
8 the home-based educational program does not meet the
9 requirements of this article.
10
11 Section 2. This act is effective July 1, 2025.
12
13 (END)
