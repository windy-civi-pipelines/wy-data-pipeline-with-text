FISCAL NOTE
The Department of Family Services indicates that this bill will create an
indeterminable administrative impact on the agency.
The fiscal impact to the judicial system is indeterminable due to an unknown number
of cases.
The Department of Administration and Information indicates there is an
indeterminable expenditure increase related to potential legal claims brought
against the State of Wyoming.
