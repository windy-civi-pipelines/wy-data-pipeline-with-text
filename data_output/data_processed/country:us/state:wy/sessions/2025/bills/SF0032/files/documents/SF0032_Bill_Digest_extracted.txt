"(f) A local authority, by resolution or ordinance at a regular meeting,
may decrease a maximum speed limit specified in W.S. 31-5-301(b)(iv) to forty-
five (45) miles per hour for an unpaved roadway within its jurisdiction without
first conducting a speed study, subject to the following conditions:
(i) Before implementing a speed reduction under this subsection,
the local authority shall consult with:
(A) The local authority’s engineer or road and bridge
superintendent; and
(B) The law enforcement agency with jurisdiction over the
roadway.
(ii) The local authority shall post the decreased speed limit upon
appropriate fixed or variable signs.
(g) Notwithstanding subsection (f) of this section, a local authority,
by resolution or ordinance at a regular meeting, may temporarily establish a
maximum speed limit lower than the limit specified in section 31-5-301(b)(iv)
for an unpaved roadway within its jurisdiction without first conducting a speed
study, subject to the following conditions:
(i) Before implementing a temporary speed reduction, the local
authority shall consult with:
(A) The local authority’s engineer or road and bridge
superintendent; and
(B) The law enforcement agency with jurisdiction over the
roadway.
(ii) The local authority shall post the decreased speed limit upon
appropriate fixed or variable signs;
(iii) In conjunction with establishing a temporary speed limit,
the local authority shall:
(A) Initiate a speed study to evaluate the appropriate long-
term speed limit; and
(B) Conduct traffic speed data collection before
implementing the temporary change to maintain data accuracy.
(iv) A temporary speed limit reduction under this subsection shall
not exceed thirty (30) days past completion of a speed study required by
paragraph (iii) of this subsection, provided that:
(A) The length of a speed study conducted by the local
authority's engineer or a private contractor shall not exceed ninety (90) days;
(B) A speed study conducted by the University of Wyoming
technology transfer center shall not exceed twelve (12) months.
(v) After the speed study required by paragraph (iii) of this
subsection is completed, the local authority shall take action to establish a
long-term speed limit;
(vi) This subsection shall not grant power to a local authority to
declare countywide maximum speed limits but shall grant power to declare maximum
speed limits for the public safety in localized geographic areas.". GERINGER
