SF0113 Braider opportunity act.
Schuler and Steinmetz and Representative(s) Andrew,
Angelos, Brady, Bratten, Lawley, Lien, Lucas,
Provenza and Rodriguez-Williams
AN ACT relating to professions and occupations; specifying that the practice of
natural hair braiding is exempt from the Wyoming Cosmetology Act; clarifying
that the owner of a salon may require certification for the practice of natural
hair braiding; making conforming amendments; and providing for an effective
date.
