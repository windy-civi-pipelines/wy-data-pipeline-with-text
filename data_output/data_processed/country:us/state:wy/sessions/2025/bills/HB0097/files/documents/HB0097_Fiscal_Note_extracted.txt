Property conveyances near critical 25LSO-0161, 1.0
infrastructure.
FISCAL NOTE
The Attorney General’s Office (A.G.) states that this bill will have a fiscal
impact in the form of increased expenditures to the Division of Criminal
Investigation (DCI). However, these increased costs are indeterminable. The A.G.
does not know the volume of investigative requests that will come to the DCI from
the Wyoming Office of Homeland Security (WOHS). The A.G. does not know how in
depth those investigations will have to be, how many of these investigations they
will be required to conduct, nor the time or resources that will be required.
NOTICE-AGENCY ESTIMATE OF ADMINISTRATIVE IMPACT REQUESTED
This bill has administrative impact that appears to increase duties or
responsibilities of one or more state agencies and may impact agency spending or
staffing requirements. As introduced, the bill does not modify any state agency
budget or current personnel authorizations.
The following state agencies will be asked to provide their estimate of the
administrative fiscal impact prior to the first committee meeting held to
consider the bill:
Office of Homeland Security
