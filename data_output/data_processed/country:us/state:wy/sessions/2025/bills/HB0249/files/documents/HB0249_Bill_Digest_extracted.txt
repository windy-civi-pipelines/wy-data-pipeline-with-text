HB0249 Runoff elections.
Rodriguez-Williams and Senator(s) Boner and McKeown
AN ACT relating to elections; requiring a runoff election after a primary
election for specified offices when no candidate receives a majority of the
vote; providing the format of the runoff ballot; changing the date for primary
elections; amending related dates; clarifying maintenance of voter registration
lists; making conforming changes; amending election contribution laws; creating
an account; providing an appropriation; requiring a report; and providing for
effective dates.
