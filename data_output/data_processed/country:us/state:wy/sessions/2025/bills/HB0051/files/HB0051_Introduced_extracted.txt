Municipal courts-maximum penalties.
Sponsored by: Joint Judiciary Interim Committee
A BILL
for
1 AN ACT relating to courts; increasing the maximum fines and
2 terms of imprisonment that municipal courts may impose; and
3 providing for an effective date.
4
5 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Wyoming:
6
7 Section 1. W.S. 5-6-201(a), 5-6-210(a) and 5-6-301(a)
8 are amended to read:
9
10 5-6-201. Jurisdiction of municipal judge; change of
11 venue prohibited; exception for certain civil penalties.
12
13 (a) Except as provided by subsection (b) of this
14 section, a municipal judge has exclusive jurisdiction over
15 all violations of ordinances of the city. The municipal
16 judge shall hear and determine violations of ordinances and

1 may impose fines not exceeding seven hundred fifty dollars
2 ($750.00) one thousand dollars ($1,000.00), or imprisonment
3 not exceeding six (6) months one (1) year, or both, to
4 which may be added costs. No change of venue shall be
5 granted in any case arising under the ordinances of the
6 city.
7
8 5-6-210. Punishment regulated by ordinance; maximum
9 fine and imprisonment.
10
11 (a) Any person convicted by a municipal judge of any
12 offense under any ordinance of the city shall be punished
13 by fine or imprisonment or both as provided by ordinance.
14 Except as provided in subsection (b) of this section, no
15 fine shall exceed seven hundred fifty dollars ($750.00) one

17 recoverable with costs, and no imprisonment shall exceed
