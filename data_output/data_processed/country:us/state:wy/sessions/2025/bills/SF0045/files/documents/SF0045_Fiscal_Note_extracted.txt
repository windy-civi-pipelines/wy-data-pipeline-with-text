Grand Total Expenditure: $25,000
Total Appropriated to Agency: $25,000
Total Appropriated by Fund:
GENERAL FUND $25,000
Description of appropriation:
The appropriation will be expended for purposes of appointing and convening a
Wyoming State Council of Defense (Council) for establishing and equipping the
Wyoming State Guard (Guard) as the Governor deems necessary. This appropriation
is only effective for one fiscal year.
It is assumed the appropriation will be expended on travel related costs for
members of the Council during Council business. It is assumed equipment, such as
laptops will be needed for Council members. The estimated expenditure of $6,250
is for the potential need for contracting with a professional services vendor by
the Council to establish the Guard.
Any additional fiscal impact, in the form of an expenditure increase, is
indeterminable. The cost to set up a Guard cannot be determined at this time
since it is dependent upon the size of the unit established and what, if any,
equipment is needed.
Members of the Guard, when serving under orders of the Governor, would receive
pay and allowances of not more than 75 percent of the same pay and allowances
authorized for personnel of equal rank in the national guard. These pay and
allowances would be funded by the legislature from General Fund appropriation or
by the Governor from the continuous appropriation for public welfare emergencies
under W.S. 9-2-1014.3.
To provide a cost estimate using the Wyoming National Guard pay and training days
as a baseline, the cost to establish a platoon (52 personnel) size force is
estimated to be at least $250,000 per year for pay and benefits, with minimal
training and only personal uniform/equipment, while excluding any other startup
costs. Startup costs would be dependent on the scope and scale of the force
capacity and desired functions of the force, and as such are indeterminable.
