Section 1:
SF0045 Wyoming state guard-amendments.
Smith, D and Representative(s) Knapp, Pendergraft and
Wharff
AN ACT relating to the Wyoming state guard; removing activation of the national
guard of Wyoming into service of the United States as a prerequisite for the
organization of the Wyoming state guard; removing federal involvement in the
Wyoming state guard; allowing the governor to organize and maintain the Wyoming
state guard; specifying the source of payment for service in the state guard;
making conforming amendments; providing an appropriation; and providing for an
effective date.
