FISCAL NOTE
This bill contains an appropriation of $400,000 from the GENERAL FUND to the
Legislative Service Office. This appropriation is effective immediately, for the
purposes of hiring not more than two full time equivalent employees.
FY 2026 FY 2027 FY 2028
NON-ADMINISTRATIVE IMPACT
Anticipated Expenditure increase
GENERAL FUND $0 $163,000 $163,000
Source of expenditure increase:
The Governor’s Office estimates the annual cost of the additional attorney
position they are requesting to carry out the requirements of the bill.
Assumptions:
The above estimate assumes an additional attorney position (title code EXMT03)
plus support costs.
The Governor’s Office request for the additional position is described in their
Agency Estimate of Administrative Impact, indicated in the standard language at
the bottom of the fiscal note.
The Attorney General’s Office indicates this bill will have an indeterminable
administrative impact, and will likely require some attorney time to advise
clients on the new rules and make sure the impacted agencies are following the
correct procedure. The Attorney General’s Office will have to revise their
rulemaking handbook and potentially some other forms as well, which will require
significant upfront time, but can probably be managed with existing resources.
Except for the appropriation, this bill is effective July 1, 2026. The
Legislative Service Office may take any action necessary to implement this act
before July 1, 2026, including the hiring of positions to implement the bill.
NOTICE-AGENCY ESTIMATE OF ADMINISTRATIVE IMPACT REQUESTED
This bill has administrative impact that appears to increase duties or
responsibilities of one or more state agencies and may impact agency spending or
staffing requirements. As introduced, the bill does not modify executive branch
state agency budget or current personnel authorizations.
The following state agencies will be asked to provide their estimate of the
administrative fiscal impact prior to the first committee meeting held to
consider the bill:
Governor's Office
Secretary of State's Office
