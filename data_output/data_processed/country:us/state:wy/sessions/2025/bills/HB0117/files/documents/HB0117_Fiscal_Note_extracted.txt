Section 1:
FISCAL NOTE
This bill contains appropriations of $14,788,031 from WATER DEVELOPMENT ACCOUNT I
to the Water Development Commission. These appropriations are effective
immediately.
This bill contains appropriations of $99,994,331 from WATER DEVELOPMENT ACCOUNT
II to the Water Development Commission. These appropriations are effective
immediately.
This bill contains an appropriation of $20,000,000 from the LEGISLATIVE
STABILIZATION RESERVE ACCOUNT to WATER DEVELOPMENT ACCOUNT II for the Goshen
Irrigation District Tunnels 1 and 2 Rehabilitation project. This appropriation is
effective immediately.
This bill contains an appropriation of $60,000,000 from the LEGISLATIVE
STABILIZATION RESERVE ACCOUNT to WATER DEVELOPMENT ACCOUNT II for the LaPrele Dam
Rehabilitation project. This appropriation shall be reduced one dollar ($1.00)
for each one dollar ($1.00) appropriated from WATER DEVELOPMENT ACCOUNT II for
the project, up to $50,000,000. The net action results in a total appropriation
of $60,000,000 ($50,000,000 from WATER DEVELOPMENT ACCOUNT II and $10,000,000
from the LEGISLATIVE STABILIZATION RESERVE ACCOUNT) for the LaPrele Dam
Rehabilitation project. This appropriation is effective immediately.
This bill contains an appropriation reduction of ($50,000,000) from WATER
DEVELOPMENT ACCOUNT III for the Alkali Creek Reservoir project.
This bill contains a transfer of $50,000,000 from WATER DEVELOPMENT ACCOUNT III
to WATER DEVELOPMENT ACCOUNT II for the LaPrele Dam Rehabilitation project.
This bill authorizes a transfer of $4,200,000 from WATER DEVELOPMENT I to WATER
DEVELOPMENT II.

Omnibus water bill-construction. 25LSO-0452, 1.0
Description of appropriations:
Most of the appropriations will be expended as 0600 series Grant & Aid Payments
to project sponsors (municipalities, irrigation districts, and special districts)
for the design, permit procurement, land acquisition, construction engineering
and construction of new water development, water rehabilitation, and water
storage projects. Funds will also be expended for weather modification cloud
seeding operations in the Wind River, Sierra Madre, and Medicine Bow Mountain
Ranges for the winter of calendar year 2025 and 2026. It is anticipated that
these funds will be expended from the 0200 series Supportive Services Costs
($5,000) and the 0900 series Contractual Services Costs ($1,192,143).
