Section 1:
Sponsored By: Select Water Committee
AN ACT relating to water development projects; authorizing construction of
designated water projects; describing projects; specifying terms and conditions
of funding for projects; providing grants; providing loans; providing
appropriations; amending the sponsor's contingency fund; amending amounts and
terms of appropriations for specified prior projects; transferring funds from
water development account I to water development account II; transferring
projects; repealing a project; authorizing one full‑time position; making
conforming amendments; and providing for an effective date.

project budget is likely to significantly supplement the available water. Upon
