Bear coupons-game and fish.
Sponsored by: Representative(s) Wharff, Rodriguez-Williams,

A BILL
for
1 AN ACT relating to game and fish; requiring the game and
2 fish commission to issue bear coupons as specified; making
3 legislative findings; requiring the game and fish
4 commission to provide evidence of grizzly bear population
5 recovery as specified; making conforming amendments;
6 requiring rulemaking; and providing for effective dates.
7
8 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Wyoming:
9
10 Section 1. W.S. 23-1-703(c), 23-2-101(j)(intro) and
11 23-3-105(a) and by creating a new subsection (d) are
12 amended to read:

5 (c) Except as provided in W.S. 23-3-105(d) and except
6 as provided in this subsection and subsection (f) of this
7 section or by rule of the commission, no person who is
8 issued a hunting license on and after July 1, 2022 for a
9 grizzly bear, or for a mountain goat, any moose or antlered
10 moose, any ram bighorn sheep, any bighorn sheep and any
11 wild bison, and no person who, on July 1, 2022, is subject

14 eligible to apply for or receive a hunting license for that

1 Yellowstone ecosystem recovery zone as established by the
2 United States fish and wildlife service shall include a
3 bear coupon. Nothing in this subsection shall prohibit the
4 issuance of a bear coupon for a hunt area that is in whole
5 or in part within any demographic monitoring area
6 established outside the greater Yellowstone ecosystem
7 recovery zone. The bear coupon shall not require an
8 additional license fee under W.S. 23-2-101(j) and shall not
9 be considered a license for purposes of W.S. 23-1-703(c). A
10 bear coupon under this subsection shall only authorize the
11 holder of the bear coupon to take not more than one (1)
12 brown or black bear or grizzly bear that is in an area
13 where the licensee would be authorized to take an elk as
14 designated by the elk license. If any licensee takes a bear
15 pursuant to a coupon under this subsection, the licensee
16 shall:
17
18 (i) Attach the bear coupon to the carcass of the
19 bear as provided in W.S. 23-3-104;
20
21 (ii) As soon as practicable, notify the game and
22 fish commission of the time, place and manner of the taking
23 of the bear;

1
2 (iii) If the bear is a grizzly bear, provide any
3 additional information required by the game and fish
4 commission to ensure that a sustainable population of
5 grizzly bears is maintained within the Yellowstone recovery
6 area.
7
8 Section 2.
9
10 (a) The legislature finds that grizzly bears have
11 recovered in Wyoming and should be removed from the
12 endangered species and threatened species list and that the
13 state should be responsible for management of grizzly bears
14 in Wyoming.
15
16 (b) Not later than July 1, 2025 and prior to issuing
17 any bear coupon in accordance with W.S. 23-3-105(d) as
18 created by section 1 of this act, the game and fish
19 commission shall present all evidence to the United States
20 fish and wildlife service supporting the fact that grizzly
21 bears have recovered in Wyoming and should be removed from
22 the endangered species and threatened species list and that
5 HB0186
2025 STATE OF WYOMING 25LSO-0449
1 the state should be responsible for management of grizzly
2 bears in Wyoming.
3
4 (c) Nothing in this act shall be deemed to allow any
5 taking of grizzly bears within the greater Yellowstone
6 ecosystem recovery zone as established by the United States
7 fish and wildlife service.
8
9 Section 3. The Wyoming game and fish commission shall
10 adopt any rules necessary to implement this act not later
11 than January 1, 2026.
12
6 HB0186
2025 STATE OF WYOMING 25LSO-0449
1 Section 4.
2
3 (a) Except as provided in subsection (b) of this
4 section, this act is effective immediately upon completion
5 of all acts necessary for a bill to become law as provided
6 by Article 4, Section 8 of the Wyoming Constitution.
7
8 (b) Section 1 of this act is effective on the earlier
9 of the following dates:
10
11 (i) Ten (10) days after the date that the
12 Wyoming game and fish commission certifies to the secretary
13 of state that grizzly bears have been removed from the
14 endangered species and threatened species list;
15
16 (ii) January 1, 2026.
