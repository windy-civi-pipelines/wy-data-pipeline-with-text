HB0031 School safety and security.
Clouston, Johnson, Kelly and Webb and Senator(s)
Rothfuss
AN ACT relating to education; requiring school districts to adopt school safety
and security policies; creating duties for the state fire marshal; providing
confidentiality requirements; and providing for an effective date.

ROLL CALL

Singh, Strock, Williams
Ayes 9 Nays 0 Excused 0 Absent 0 Conflicts 0
1/28/2025 H Placed on General File
HB0031HS001/ADOPTED
Page 1-line 14 After "." insert "Not later than school year 2026-2027,
all door securement devices utilized in any public
school building shall be from the list of approved door
securement devices published by the state fire marshal
pursuant to W.S. 35-9-107(d).".
Page 3-lines 6 through 12 Delete entirely and insert:
"(d) Not later than July 1, 2025, the state fire marshal shall provide
the board of trustees in each school district with a list of door securement
devices appropriate to be utilized in K-12 public school buildings. The state
fire marshal may update the list of approved devices annually.".
Page 3-line 14 Delete "July 1, 2025." and insert "immediately upon
completion of all acts necessary for a bill to become
law as provided by Article 4, Section 8 of the Wyoming
Constitution.". ANDREW, CHAIRMAN
