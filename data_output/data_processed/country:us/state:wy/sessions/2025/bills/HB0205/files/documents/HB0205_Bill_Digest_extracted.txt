Section 1:
HB0205 Wyoming state fair department.
Johnson, Lien and Winter and Senator(s) Boner and
Smith, D
AN ACT relating to the administration of the government; creating the Wyoming
state fair department from the state fair division within the department of
agriculture; providing for the appointment of a director; removing the state
fair functions and division from the department of agriculture for transfer to
the Wyoming state fair department; authorizing the transfer of positions and
funds; providing for subsequent budgeting for the Wyoming state fair department;
making conforming amendments; and providing for an effective date.
