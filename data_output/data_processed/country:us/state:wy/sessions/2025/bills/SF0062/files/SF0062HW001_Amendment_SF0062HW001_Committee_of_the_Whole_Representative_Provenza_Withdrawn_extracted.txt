9 21-13-101. Definitions.
10
11 (a) As used in this chapter:
12
13 (i) "Average daily membership" or "ADM" means the
14 aggregate number of pupils present plus the aggregate number of
15 pupils absent, divided by the actual number of days the school is
16 in session for the year. Pupils who attend at least eighty percent
17 (80%) seventy-five percent (75%) of a full time equivalency basis
18 shall be considered full time. For pupils enrolled in school on
19 less than eighty percent (80%) seventy-five percent (75%) of a
20 full time equivalency basis, the school district shall calculate
21 the pupil's contribution to the ADM on a prorated basis with the
22 hours or class periods of enrollment being the numerator and the
23 hours or class periods of full time equivalency being the
24 denominator. Pupils who have withdrawn from school or who have
25 been absent for more than ten (10) consecutive calendar days shall
26 not be counted as members;
27
28 Section 4. Section 3 of this act shall apply beginning school
