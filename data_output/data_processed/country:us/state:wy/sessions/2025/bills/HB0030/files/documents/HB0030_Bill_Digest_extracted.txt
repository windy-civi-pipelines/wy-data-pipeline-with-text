HB0030 Driver's licenses and IDs-revisions.
Interim Committee
AN ACT relating to motor vehicles; amending expiration and renewal of licenses
for persons under twenty-one (21) years of age; amending identification card
contents; clarifying the timeline for contested case hearings regarding
licenses; amending requirements for replacement licenses; adding a fee for
replacement licenses; repealing obsolete provisions; providing rulemaking
authority; and providing for effective dates.
