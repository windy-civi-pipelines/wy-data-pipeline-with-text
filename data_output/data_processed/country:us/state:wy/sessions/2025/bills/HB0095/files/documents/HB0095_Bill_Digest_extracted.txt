Section 1:
HB0095 School generational account.
AN ACT relating to state funds; creating an inviolate school generational
account within the permanent land fund; creating the school generational income
account; providing for the long-term investment of the school generational
account as specified; providing for expenditure of the account's earnings for
the benefit of schools; providing an appropriation; and providing for an
effective date.
