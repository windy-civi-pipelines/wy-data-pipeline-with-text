Educational bankruptcy act. 25LSO-0347, 1.2
FISCAL NOTE
The fiscal impact to the Department of Education is indeterminable and may have a
significant administrative impact beginning May 1, 2027, depending on the number
of school districts determined to be educationally bankrupt under this bill.
The fiscal impact to the judicial system is indeterminable due to an unknown number
of cases.
Prepared by: Matthew Willmarth, LSO Phone: 777-7881
(Information provided by Trent Carroll, Department of Education, 777-7720; Claire
Smith, Supreme Court, 777-7502)
