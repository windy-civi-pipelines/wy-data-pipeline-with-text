Section 1:
2025 STATE OF WYOMING 25LSO-0022
SENATE FILE NO. SF0078
Sponsored by: Senator(s) Hutchings, Biteman and Ide and
Representative(s) Knapp and Singh
A BILL
for
1 AN ACT relating to elections; prohibiting a person from
2 distributing unsolicited absentee ballot application forms
3 to any qualified elector as specified; and providing for an
4 effective date.
5
6 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Wyoming:
7
8 Section 1. W.S. 22-9-104 by creating a new subsection
9 (e) is amended to read:
10
11 22-9-104. How to apply; information required.
12
13 (e) No person, except a county clerk, the secretary
14 of state or their designees, shall distribute an absentee
15 ballot request form to any qualified elector unless that
1 SF0078
2025 STATE OF WYOMING 25LSO-0022
1 qualified elector specifically solicits an absentee ballot
2 request form. For purposes of this subsection, "absentee
3 ballot request form" includes any document that claims to
4 be or that is deceptively similar to an official election
5 document and that is provided to the qualified elector by
6 mail, email or any other means.
7
8 Section 2. This act is effective immediately upon
9 completion of all acts necessary for a bill to become law
10 as provided by Article 4, Section 8 of the Wyoming
11 Constitution.
12
13 (END)
