Section 1:
Sponsored By: Representative(s) Lien, Allemand, Angelos, Bear,
Brady, Campbell, K, Guggenmos, Heiner, Hoeft, McCann,
Pendergraft and Webb and Senator(s) Pearson
AN ACT relating to common law, statutes and rules of construction; specifying
definitions and standards for the application of a person's biological sex in
law, rules or regulations; providing for the law to distinguish between
accommodations for males and females; requiring the collection of vital
statistics and other data to identify persons as male or female at birth; and
providing for an effective date.

had, but for a congenital anomaly or intentional or unintentional disruption,
