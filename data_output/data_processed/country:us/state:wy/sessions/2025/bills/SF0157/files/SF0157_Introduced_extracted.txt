1 AN ACT relating to the department of family services;

3 family services to review the effectiveness and impact of
4 the department's programs; authorizing the sharing of data;
5 creating exceptions for the sharing of confidential
6 information; requiring rulemaking; requiring a report; and
7 providing for effective dates.
8

2 35-20-108. Records confidential; exception.
3
4 Except as provided under W.S. 9-2-2104(a)(x) and 35-20-116,
5 records of the department or other agency or the court
6 pertaining to a vulnerable adult receiving protective
7 services under this act are not open to public inspection.
8 Information contained in those records shall not be
9 disclosed to the public in any manner that will identify
10 any individual. The records may be made available for
11 inspection only upon application to the court pursuant to
12 W.S. 35-20-112 for good cause shown.
13
14 42-2-111. Prohibited disclosure and use of records;
15 penalty; judicial discovery.
16
17 (a) Except as provided in this section, and by W.S.
18 9-2-2104(a)(x) to the extent allowed by federal law, no
19 person shall disclose, receive, use or knowingly permit or
20 participate in the use of any information derived from
21 records maintained pursuant to law or acquired in the
22 performance of duties under this article for purposes not
23 directly related to the administration of this article.

2 Section 2. The department of family services shall
3 promulgate all rules necessary to implement the provisions
4 of this act.
5
6 Section 3. The department of family services shall
7 report to the joint judiciary interim committee on the
8 types of data that are shared pursuant to this act and any
9 safeguards that have been implemented to protect personal
10 data not later than October 1, 2025.
11
12 Section 4.
13
14 (a) Except as provided in subsection (b) of this
15 section, this act is effective immediately upon completion
16 of all acts necessary for a bill to become law as provided
17 by Article 4, Section 8 of the Wyoming Constitution.
18
19 (b) Section 1 of this act is effective July 1, 2025.
