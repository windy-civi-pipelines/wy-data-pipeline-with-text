Section 1:
2025 STATE OF WYOMING 25LSO-0702
Manufacturers and dealers of new trailers-changes.
Sponsored by: Representative(s) Tarver, Brown, L, Campbell,
K and Geringer and Senator(s) Anderson,
Brennan and Cooper
A BILL
for
1 AN ACT relating to motor vehicles; requiring applicants for
2 motor vehicle dealer licenses who sell trailers to submit a
3 sales authorization agreement; exempting motor vehicle
4 dealers and manufacturers who sell trailers from specified
5 provisions; providing definitions; and providing for an
6 effective date.
7
8 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Wyoming:
9
10 Section 1. W.S. 31-16-101(a) by creating a new
11 paragraph (xxxii), 31-16-103(a) by creating a new paragraph
12 (ix) and 31-16-124 by creating a new subsection (g) are
13 amended to read:
14
15 31-16-101. Definitions.
1 HB0242
2025 STATE OF WYOMING 25LSO-0702
1
2 (a) As used in this act:
3
4 (xxxii) "Sales authorization" means a document
5 from a manufacturer or distributor of trailers as defined
6 by W.S. 31-1-101(a)(xxiii) that does not contract through
7 enfranchisement, authorizing a dealer to engage in the
8 business of selling any specified make of trailer other
9 than a house trailer as defined by W.S.
10 31-1-101(a)(xxiii)(A) or a semitrailer as defined by W.S.
11 31-1-101(a)(xxiii)(B).
12
13 31-16-103. Licenses; applications; issuance,
14 suspension and revocation; change in ownership; rulemaking.
15
16 (a) An applicant for a license required under this
17 act shall, before commencing business and annually
18 thereafter submit an application to the department in a
19 form prescribed by the department containing the following:
20
21 (ix) For a new vehicle dealer's license to sell
22 trailers as defined by W.S. 31-1-101(a)(xxiii), if the
23 sales agreement does not involve enfranchisement, a sales
2 HB0242
2025 STATE OF WYOMING 25LSO-0702
1 authorization from the manufacturer or distributor stating
2 that the dealer is authorized to sell any specific make of
3 new trailer other than a house trailer as defined by W.S.
4 31-1-101(a)(xxiii)(A) or a semitrailer as defined by W.S.
5 31-1-101(a)(xxiii)(B).
6
7 31-16-124. Applicability of provisions.
8
9 (g) The provisions of W.S. 31-16-109 through
10 31-16-111, 31-16-113, 31-16-114 and 31-16-117 shall not
11 apply to dealers or manufacturers of new trailers other
12 than a house trailer as defined by W.S.
13 31-1-101(a)(xxiii)(A) or a semitrailer as defined by W.S.
14 31-1-101(a)(xxiii)(B). The provisions of W.S. 31-16-108
15 shall not apply to manufacturers of new trailers other than
16 a house trailer as defined by W.S. 31-1-101(a)(xxiii)(A) or
17 a semitrailer as defined by W.S. 31-1-101(a)(xxiii)(B).
18
19 Section 2. This act is effective July 1, 2025.
20
21 (END)
