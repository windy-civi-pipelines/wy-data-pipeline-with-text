Section 1:
SF0099 Game and fish property tax exemption.
Resources Interim Committee
AN ACT relating to taxation and revenue; exempting real property owned by the
Wyoming game and fish commission from property taxation except for property
that is used for wildlife management purposes or employee residences and subject
to special taxation; amending a special tax on real property owned by the
Wyoming game and fish commission that is used for any other purpose; specifying
applicability; and providing for an effective date.
