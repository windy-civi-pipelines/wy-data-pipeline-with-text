revisions.
FISCAL NOTE
FY 2026 FY 2027 FY 2028
NON-ADMINISTRATIVE IMPACT
Anticipated Expenditure increase
HIGHWAY FUND $67,000 $0 $0
Source of expenditure increase:
This bill requires the Wyoming Department of Transportation to issue driver’s
licenses and identification cards stating “Not a United States citizen” to a person
who is not a United States citizen but who has lawful status as defined by Title
6, Section 37.3 of the Code of Federal Regulation. The Wyoming Department of
Transportation anticipates it will need to make programming updates to the driver
licensing issuance system and modify the layout of driver’s licenses and
identification cards to accommodate the required language, resulting in an
expenditure increase to the Highway Fund of $67,000 in FY 2026.
Assumptions:
The expenditure increase reflected above could be considered an administrative
cost. However, for simplicity and to follow consistent practice on legislation of
this type, it is included on the fiscal note
