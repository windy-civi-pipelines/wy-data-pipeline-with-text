Section 1:
SF0105
Sovereignty.
Sponsored By: Senator(s) Ide, Laursen, D, McKeown, Pearson and
Smith, D and Representative(s) Allemand, Banks,
Smith, S, Strock, Wharff and Winter
AN ACT relating to property, conveyances and security transactions; prohibiting
persons from selling or transferring real property to the federal government as
specified; providing the manner by which a person may sell or transfer real
property to the federal government; providing a civil penalty for failing to
timely submit information to the legislature; providing definitions; specifying
applicability; requiring rulemaking; and providing for an effective date.
