HB0116
restrictions.
Webber and Winter and Senator(s) Kolb, Laursen, D and

AN ACT relating to driver's licenses; specifying that driver's licenses and
driving operating privilege cards issued by other jurisdictions to unauthorized
aliens are invalid in Wyoming; providing definitions; providing a penalty;
making conforming amendments; requiring rulemaking; and provide for effective
dates.
