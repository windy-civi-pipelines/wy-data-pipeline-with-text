Section 1:
Fishing outfitters and guides-registration of
HB0005
fishing boats.
Resources Interim Committee
AN ACT relating to game and fish; authorizing the Wyoming game and fish
commission to regulate commercially guided fishing boats; providing for
registration of commercially guided fishing boats, vehicles and trailers
operated by outfitters and professional fishing guides; providing an exception;
creating an advisory board; specifying duties of the advisory board; requiring
a report; providing definitions; providing rulemaking authority; and providing
for an effective date.

HB0005HS001/ADOPTED
Page 1-line 5 After "guides;" insert "providing an exception;
creating an advisory board; specifying duties of the
advisory board; requiring a report;".
Page 1-line 14 After "authority;" insert "advisory board;".
Page 2-line 6 Delete "state." and insert "state in a lawful ratio
that prioritizes Wyoming resident professional guides
and outfitters. This subsection shall not apply to a
Wyoming resident if that person uses a city, town,
county or state boat ramp or other access point to
launch a commercially guided fishing boat to guide
fishermen if the Wyoming resident is guiding the
fishermen to a location where the lands are owned or
leased by the Wyoming resident or the Wyoming
resident's immediate family member.".
Page 2-after line 6 Insert the following and renumber as necessary:
"(b) There is created a commercially guided fishing boats advisory board.
The board shall consist of seven (7) members appointed by the commission. One
(1) member shall be appointed from each appointment district pursuant to W.S.
9-1-218. Members shall serve a four (4) year term. The commission may remove
any member of the board, subject to W.S. 9-1-202. Vacancies of the board shall
be filled by appointment for the unexpired term. The board shall provide
recommendations to the commission for promulgating rules under subsection (c)
of this section.".
Page 2-line 8 Delete "(b)" and insert "(c)".
Page 2-line 19 After "regulations." insert "The annual fee established
under this subsection may be different for resident and
nonresident professional fishing guides or outfitters
and shall be in a ratio similar to the fee ratio for
(12) month resident fishing licenses and (12) month
nonresident fishing licenses under W.S. 23-2-201(d).".
Page 3-line 1 Delete "(c)" and insert "(d)".
Page 4-after line 2 Insert the following and renumber as necessary:
"Section 2. Not later than November 1, 2025, the game and fish commission
and the commercially guided fishing boats advisory board created in section 1
of this act shall make recommendations to the joint travel, recreation, wildlife
and cultural resources interim committee on the creation of a new board or
amendment to an existing board to carry out the duties specified in section 1
of this act.".
Page 2-line 4 Delete "Section 2." and insert "Section 3.". BYRON,
CHAIRMAN
