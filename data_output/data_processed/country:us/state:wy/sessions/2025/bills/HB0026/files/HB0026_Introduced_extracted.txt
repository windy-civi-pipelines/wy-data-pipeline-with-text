Section 1:
2025 STATE OF WYOMING 25LSO-0076
Highway safety-child restraints.
Sponsored by: Joint Transportation, Highways & Military
Affairs Interim Committee
A BILL
for
1 AN ACT relating to motor vehicles; requiring rear-facing
2 child safety restraint systems for children younger than
3 two (2) years old or under the weight or height limits
4 specified for the particular child safety restraint system;
5 clarifying the placement of child safety restraint systems
6 in front of active airbags; and providing for an effective
7 date.
8
9 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Wyoming:
10
11 Section 1. W.S. 31-5-1303(a) is amended to read:
12
13 31-5-1303. Child safety restraint system; required
14 use; exception.
15
1 HB0026
2025 STATE OF WYOMING 25LSO-0076
1 (a) Except as otherwise provided in subsection (b) of
2 this section, no person shall operate a passenger vehicle
3 in this state unless each child who is a passenger in that
4 vehicle and who has not reached his ninth birthday nine (9)
5 years of age is properly secured in a child safety
6 restraint system in a seat of the vehicle other than the
7 front seat, except if the vehicle is only equipped with one
8 (1) row of seats, or if all safety belts in the rows of
9 seats behind the front seat are in use by other child
10 passengers in the vehicle, the a child may be properly
11 secured in a child safety restraint system in the front
12 passenger seat of the vehicle, except that a rear-facing
13 infant seat child safety restraint system shall not be
14 placed in front of an active airbag. The child safety
15 restraint system required by this subsection shall be
16 rear-facing until the child reaches either the age of two
17 (2) or the maximum weight or height limit specified by the
18 manufacturer of the child safety restraint system for rear-
19 facing installation.
20
21 Section 2. This act is effective July 1, 2025.
22
23 (END)
