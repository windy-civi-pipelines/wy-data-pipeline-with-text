Section 1:
Speed limits established by local authorities-
HB0313
amendments.
Winter and Wylie and Senator(s) Pearson
AN ACT relating to speed limits; changing the process for counties to decrease
maximum speed limits on highways as specified; providing an exception for the
department of transportation's approval of alterations on state highways as
specified; authorizing the transportation commission to override a board of
county commissioners' resolution to decrease maximum speed limits as specified;
making conforming amendments; specifying applicability; and providing for an
effective date.
