Section 1:
FISCAL NOTE
The fiscal impact is indeterminable.
This bill directs the State Superintendent of Public Instruction to prepare a plan
for reorganization of the State Board of Education and State Board of Career and
Technical Education. Subject to approval of the reorganization plan by act of the
Legislature, the State Superintendent shall be the successor to each board and
would provide for the transfer of all personnel, supplies, equipment, and
appropriations for each board to the State Superintendent. The fiscal impact would
be dependent upon the reorganization plan ultimately approved by the Legislature.
