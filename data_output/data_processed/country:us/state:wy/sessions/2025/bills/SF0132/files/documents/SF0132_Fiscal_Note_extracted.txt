FISCAL NOTE
There is no direct fiscal impact to the Wyoming Military Department. However, if
this bill passes, the Wyoming National Guard may (or may not) have the capacity
or ability to mobilize and provide a response to all situations identified in
this bill. As with any mobilization of the Wyoming Nation Guard, all
mobilizations for this bill will involve federal expenditures that will require
reimbursement with state funding following established fiscal processes. Any
administrative state expenditure by the Wyoming Military Department for
mobilization as a result of this legislation will be from existing state
appropriations as conduct of the normal operations of the Department.
The fiscal impact to the judicial system is indeterminable due to an unknown
number of cases.
No significant fiscal or personnel impact to the Governor’s Office.
