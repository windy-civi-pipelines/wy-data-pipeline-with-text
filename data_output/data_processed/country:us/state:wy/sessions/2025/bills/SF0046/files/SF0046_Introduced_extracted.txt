for
1 AN ACT relating to water and sewer districts; increasing
2 the cost requirements for water, sewer and water and sewer
3 district purchases or projects that are required to be
4 submitted for bids; specifying applicability; and providing
5 for an effective date.
6
7 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Wyoming:
8
9 Section 1. W.S. 41-10-113(a)(xi) is amended to read:
10
11 41-10-113. Powers of district board.
12
13 (a) For and on behalf of the district the board of
14 any district shall have the following powers:

1 (xi) Except as otherwise provided in this act,
2 to enter into contracts and agreements affecting the

4 contracts with the United States of America and any of its

6 municipality or district for the operation of a common or
7 jointly owned project. Any improvement or improvements of
8 any nature made in any district where the entire cost,
9 value or amount of such work including labor and materials
10 shall exceed thirty thousand dollars ($30,000.00)
11 seventy-five thousand dollars ($75,000.00), except such
12 work done by employees of the district with supplies and
13 materials purchased by it as hereinafter provided or except
14 by labor or supplies and materials, or all of such,

16 the state of Wyoming, or any federal or state agency,

19 to be entered into by the district with the lowest

2 sum or unit basis. No contract shall be entered into for

4 with a sufficient surety or sureties approved by the board
5 and in an amount fixed by the board for the faithful
6 performance of the contract. Upon default in the
7 performance of any contract, the proper official may
8 advertise and relet the remainder of the work without

2 bidding requirements of this paragraph shall not apply to

7 Section 2. This act shall apply to any improvement or
