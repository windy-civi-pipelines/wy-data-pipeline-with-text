FISCAL NOTE
This bill modifies the Cease and Transfer Priority List to update the costs
associated with projects that remain on the list and to remove 2 projects from
the list. This bill removes 2 projects from the list as they received funding and
increases the expected costs of 1 facility based on updated cost estimate.
The current remaining project cost estimates for the 14 projects on the list are
$26,458,303 for 6 transfer station projects and 8 closure projects.
As of September 2024, 37 projects have been funded under the Program; 13 transfer
Stations, 23 landfill closures and 1 carcass management project. The amount of
funds granted and/or loaned by the State Lands Investment Board for projects
totals $52,864,231.50; with $46,797,749.50 being allocated in grants and
$6,066,482 in loans. Remaining funds available for projects total $23,514,167.96;
with $16,433,993.79 in grants and $7,080,174.17 in loans.
