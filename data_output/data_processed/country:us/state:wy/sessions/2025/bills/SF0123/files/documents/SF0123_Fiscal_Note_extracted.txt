FISCAL NOTE
This bill contains an appropriation of $4,950,000 from the PUBLIC SCHOOL FOUNDATION
PROGRAM ACCOUNT to the Department of Education - School Finance. This appropriation
is effective immediately.
DETAIL OF APPROPRIATION
Agency #: 205 Agency Name: Department of Education - School Finance
Unit: 4500 Public School Crossing Guard Program
EXPENDITURE BY SERIES AND YEAR FY 2025 FY 2026 FY 2027
0600 Grant & Aid Payments $1,650,000 $1,650,000 $1,650,000
Total Expenditure Per Year: $1,650,000 $1,650,000 $1,650,000
Grand Total Expenditure: $4,950,000
Total Appropriated to Agency: $4,950,000
Total Appropriated by Fund:
PUBLIC SCHOOL FOUNDATION PROGRAM ACCOUNT $4,950,000
Description of appropriation:
This appropriation will be expended on grants to Wyoming school districts for school
years 2025-26, 2026-27, and 2027-28, for state assistance, training and education
for volunteers and faculty, wages for faculty, and equipment for a crossing guard
program at schools within the school district.
A school district may apply to the Wyoming Department of Education (WDE) for a
grant, not to exceed $10,000 per school and the total awards shall not exceed
$1,650,000 per school year. The WDE indicates the final $1,650,000 will be expended
in FY 2028.
The WDE also indicates that on its own, this bill will not require additional
personnel or funding. However, passage of multiple bills of this type may result
in a cumulative administrative impact to the WDE.
