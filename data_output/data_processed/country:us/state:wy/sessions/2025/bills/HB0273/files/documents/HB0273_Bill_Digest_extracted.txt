Section 1:
Sponsored By: Representative(s) Rodriguez-Williams, Angelos,
Haroldson, Harshman, Hoeft, Johnson and Ottman and
Senator(s) Biteman, Boner, Brennan and French
AN ACT relating to public health and safety; providing legislative findings;
prohibiting the state and specified governmental entities from adopting any
law, rule or policy that targets pregnancy centers for oversight or regulation
based on the centers' stance against abortion; providing definitions; providing
penalties; specifying available damages; providing a right of intervention for
members of the legislature as specified; making conforming amendments; and
providing for an effective date.
