HB0237 License plate issuance.
Filer, Geringer, Larsen, L, Larson, JT, Lawley,
Neiman, Tarver, Thayer and Williams and Senator(s)
Crum, Jones, Landen and McKeown
AN ACT relating to motor vehicles; requiring license plates to be issued by the
department of transportation; clarifying the number of license plates to be
provided; removing the requirement that license plates be replaced after a
specified time; making conforming amendments; requiring rulemaking; and
providing for effective dates.
