Section 1:
2025 STATE OF WYOMING 25LSO-0036
SENATE FILE NO. SF0074
Immunity for drug overdose reporting.
Committee
A BILL
for
1 AN ACT relating to controlled substances; providing
2 immunity from prosecution of specified offenses under the
3 Wyoming Controlled Substances Act of 1971 to persons who
4 report or experience an emergency drug overdose event as
5 specified; providing applicability; making conforming
6 amendments; and providing for an effective date.
7
8 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Wyoming:
9
10 Section 1. W.S. 35-7-1064 is created to read:
11
12 35-7-1064. Drug overdose; limited immunity from
13 prosecution.
14
1 SF0074
2025 STATE OF WYOMING 25LSO-0036
1 (a) A person who, in good faith, seeks medical
2 assistance for himself or another person is immune from
3 criminal prosecution for an offense listed in subsection
4 (c) of this section if:
5
6 (i) The person reasonably believes he or another
7 person is experiencing a drug overdose event;
8
9 (ii) The person provides a description of the
10 actual location of the drug overdose event;
11
12 (iii) The person remains at the scene of the
13 drug overdose event until a responding law enforcement
14 officer or emergency medical service provider arrives, or
15 the person remains at the medical care facility where the
16 person experiencing the drug overdose event is located
17 until a responding law enforcement officer arrives;
18
19 (iv) The person cooperates with a responding law
20 enforcement officer, emergency medical service provider or
21 medical care provider, including by providing information
22 about the person experiencing the drug overdose event and
2 SF0074
2025 STATE OF WYOMING 25LSO-0036
1 the type of substance that may have caused the drug
2 overdose event; and
3
4 (v) The offense listed in subsection (c) of this
5 section arises from the same course of events as the drug
6 overdose event.
7
8 (b) The immunity from prosecution under subsection
9 (a) of this section shall extend to the person experiencing
10 the drug overdose event if all the conditions specified in
11 subsection (a) of this section are satisfied.
12
13 (c) The immunity from prosecution under subsection
14 (a) of this section shall apply to the following offenses:
15
16 (i) Possession of a controlled substance under
17 W.S. 35-7-1031(c);
18
19 (ii) Use of a controlled substance under W.S.
20 35-7-1039.
21
22 (d) A person experiencing a drug overdose event shall
23 only be eligible to receive immunity from prosecution under
3 SF0074
2025 STATE OF WYOMING 25LSO-0036
1 this section not more than two (2) times in a twelve (12)
2 month period. The limitations under this subsection shall
3 not apply to the person who seeks medical assistance for
4 himself or another person in accordance with subsection (a)
5 of this section.
6
7 (e) As used in this section:
8
9 (i) "Drug overdose event" means a condition,
10 including extreme physical illness, a decreased level of
11 consciousness or respiratory depression that a reasonable
12 person would believe requires medical assistance to address
13 and correct resulting from the consumption or use of a
14 controlled substance or another substance combined with a
15 controlled substance;
16
17 (ii) "Good faith" does not include seeking
18 medical assistance or immunity under this section as a
19 result of using a controlled substance during the course of
20 a law enforcement agency's execution of a search warrant,
21 arrest warrant or other lawful search or arrest.
22
23 (f) Nothing in this section shall be interpreted to:
4 SF0074
2025 STATE OF WYOMING 25LSO-0036
1
2 (i) Prohibit the criminal prosecution of a
3 person for an offense other than the offenses specified in
4 subsection (c) of this section;
5
6 (ii) Limit the authority of a law enforcement
7 officer to obtain or use evidence obtained from a report,
8 recording or other statement provided by a person seeking
9 medical assistance for himself or another person under this
10 section to investigate and prosecute an offense other than
11 the offenses listed in subsection (c) of this section.
12
13 Section 2. W.S. 35-7-1002(a)(xxviii),
14 35-7-1031(c)(intro) and 35-7-1039 are amended to read:
15
16 35-7-1002. Definitions.
17
18 (a) As used in this act:
19
20 (xxviii) "This act" means W.S. 35-7-1001 through
21 35-7-1063 35-7-1064.
22
5 SF0074
2025 STATE OF WYOMING 25LSO-0036
1 35-7-1031. Unlawful manufacture or delivery;
2 counterfeit substance; unlawful possession.
3
4 (c) Except as provided in W.S. 35-7-1064, it is
5 unlawful for any person knowingly or intentionally to
6 possess a controlled substance unless the substance was
7 obtained directly from, or pursuant to a valid prescription
8 or order of a practitioner while acting in the course of
9 his professional practice, or except as otherwise
10 authorized by this act. With the exception of any drug that
11 has received final approval from the United States food and
12 drug administration, including dronabinol as listed in W.S.
13 35-7-1018(h), and notwithstanding any other provision of
14 this act, no practitioner shall dispense or prescribe
15 marihuana, tetrahydrocannabinol, or synthetic equivalents
16 of marihuana or tetrahydrocannabinol. No prescription or
17 practitioner's order for marihuana, tetrahydrocannabinol,
18 or synthetic equivalents of marihuana or
19 tetrahydrocannabinol shall be valid, unless the
20 prescription is for a drug that has received final approval
21 from the United States food and drug administration,
22 including dronabinol. Any person who violates this
23 subsection:
6 SF0074
2025 STATE OF WYOMING 25LSO-0036
1
2 35-7-1039. Person using or under influence of
3 controlled substance.
4
5 Except as provided in W.S. 35-7-1064, any person who
6 knowingly or intentionally uses or is under the influence
7 of a controlled substance listed in Schedules I, II or III
8 except when administered or prescribed by or under the
9 direction of a licensed practitioner, shall be guilty of a
10 misdemeanor and shall be punished by imprisonment in the
11 county jail not to exceed six (6) months or a fine not to
12 exceed seven hundred fifty dollars ($750.00), or by both.
13
14 Section 3. This act is effective July 1, 2025.
15
16 (END)
