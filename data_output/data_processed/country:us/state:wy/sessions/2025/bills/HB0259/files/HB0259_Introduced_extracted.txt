A BILL
for
1 AN ACT relating to school building and facility projects;

3 fiscal biennium commencing July 1, 2024 and ending June 30,
4 2026; specifying conditions for expenditures;
5 reappropriating funds; and providing for an effective date.
6
7 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Wyoming:
8
9 Section 1.
10
11 (a) This section shall consist of funds appropriated
12 for K-12 school building and facility needs.
13
14 (b) As used in this section a "priority" identified
15 by an Arabic numeral means the project ranking as submitted

1 by the school facilities commission within its budget
2 recommendations provided under W.S. 21-15-119.
3
4 (c) Each amount appropriated from the public school
5 foundation program account for a school facility project
6 under subsection (g) of this section shall remain in effect
7 from the effective date of the appropriation until that
8 project is completed, unless otherwise provided by law.
9 Upon completion of a project specified in subsection (g) of
10 this section, any unexpended, unobligated funds remaining
11 from the appropriation for the project shall revert to the
12 public school foundation program account.
13
14 (d) An estimated schedule for deploying projects
15 funded by this act, as adopted by the school facilities
16 commission and as contained within the 2025-2026 biennial
17 budget, inclusive of the supplemental budget request,
18 submitted by the commission under W.S. 21-15-119, and
19 further refined by the final, corrected budget submitted by
20 the governor's office, shall be used by the state
21 construction department to the extent practicable to guide
22 expenditure of appropriated funds, subject to restrictions
23 contained in W.S. 21-15-119(c). The estimated schedule

2025 STATE OF WYOMING 25LSO-0596
1 shall be based upon information, processes, events and
2 expenditures and shall not be binding upon the state
3 construction department or the school facilities
4 commission.
5
6 (e) Amounts appropriated under subsection (g) of this
7 section or included in the budget requests submitted by the
8 school facilities commission or the governor's office shall
9 not be construed to be an entitlement or guaranteed amount
10 and shall be expended by the school facilities commission
11 to ensure adequate, efficient and the most cost effective
12 school buildings and facilities in accordance with W.S.
13 21-15-114(a)(vii) and 21-15-117(b) that are contained in
14 school district facility plans as required by W.S.
15 21-15-116.
16
17 (f) In addition to accounting and reporting
18 requirements imposed under W.S. 28-11-301(c)(iv), the state
19 construction department shall report monthly on the
20 deployment of amounts to fund projects under this section
21 in accordance with the deployment schedule, information on
22 project progression and, if applicable, the rationale for
23 any deviation from the estimated schedule. The reports

4 appropriations for inflationary costs associated with
5 projects approved by the commission and funded by the
6 legislature. The original scope and design of the projects
7 for which these funds are expended shall not be modified as
8 a result of the expenditure of the funds reappropriated
9 under this subsection. This reappropriation shall not be
10 transferred or expended for any other purpose. The
11 commission shall report the expenditure of funds
12 reappropriated under this subsection in the manner required
13 under subsection (f) of this section.
14
15 Section 2. This act is effective immediately upon

17 as provided by Article 4, Section 8 of the Wyoming
18 Constitution.
