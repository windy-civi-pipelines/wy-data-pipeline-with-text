Section 1:
FISCAL NOTE
This fiscal impact in the form of revenue decreases from trailer registration fees
to the Public School Foundation Program Account (SFP) and school districts is
indeterminable and may require the Legislature to appropriate additional funds from
the SFP to ensure sufficient funding is available for school districts to offset
any reduction in revenue generated by the trailer registration fees.
Any additional fiscal or personnel impact is not determinable due to insufficient
time to complete the fiscal note process.
