HB0310 Trailer permanent registration-amendments.
Haroldson, Knapp, Schmid, Wharff and Wylie and
Senator(s) Crago and Ide
AN ACT relating to motor vehicles; amending eligibility requirements for
permanent registrations of a trailer; providing for the permanent registration
of trailers that qualify as commercial vehicles as specified; providing a fee
exemption for permanently registered trailers as specified; making conforming
amendments; specifying applicability; and providing for an effective date.
