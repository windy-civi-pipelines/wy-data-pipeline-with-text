Section 1:
2025 STATE OF WYOMING 25LSO-0393
Protecting religious assembly in states of emergency act.
Sponsored by: Representative(s) Smith, S and Senator(s)
Hutchings
A BILL
for
1 AN ACT relating to public health and safety; requiring
2 rules enacted during public health emergencies to provide
3 exemptions for religious assembly as specified; providing
4 for enforcement and remedies; authorizing a cause of
5 action; providing exceptions to the Wyoming Governmental
6 Claims Act; making conforming amendments; and providing for
7 an effective date.
8
9 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Wyoming:
10
11 Section 1. W.S. 35-4-116 is created to read:
12
13 35-4-116. Public health emergencies; religious
14 assembly exemptions.
15
1 HB0114
2025 STATE OF WYOMING 25LSO-0393
1 (a) For any order, rule, regulation, policy or
2 directive issued during a public health emergency as
3 defined by W.S. 35-4-115(a)(i) that requires the closure of
4 or otherwise limits a business or other entity from
5 remaining open and that exempts, in whole or in part, any
6 business or entity from those closure requirements or
7 limitations, the order, rule, regulation, policy or
8 directive shall provide the same exemption to:
9
10 (i) Any place of worship qualifying as an exempt
11 organization for religious purposes under section 501(c)(3)
12 of the federal Internal Revenue Code;
13
14 (ii) Churches, their integrated auxiliaries and
15 conventions or associations of churches;
16
17 (iii) Any gathering for religious assembly in
18 any place otherwise open for religious assembly, including
19 those taking place in private residences and public places.
20
21 (b) This section shall be construed to afford to
22 religious institutions, places of worship and religious
23 assemblies the same degree of freedom to meet during public
2 HB0114
2025 STATE OF WYOMING 25LSO-0393
1 health emergencies as is afforded to any business or entity
2 that is exempted from closure requirements implemented
3 during a public health emergency.
4
5 (c) Any person aggrieved by a violation of this
6 section may bring a civil action for relief in any court of
7 competent jurisdiction. A court may, for an act or practice
8 that violates this section:
9
10 (i) Award damages, costs and attorney fees;
11
12 (ii) Issue a declaratory judgment for or enjoin
13 an act or practice that violates this section;
14
15 (iii) Award exemplary or punitive damages, if
16 the act or practice that violates this section was wanton
17 or malicious.
18
19 Section 2. W.S. 1-39-104(a), 1-39-118(d) and
20 1-42-202(f) are amended to read:
21
22 1-39-104. Granting immunity from tort liability;
23 liability on contracts; exceptions.
3 HB0114
2025 STATE OF WYOMING 25LSO-0393
1
2 (a) A governmental entity and its public employees
3 while acting within the scope of duties are granted
4 immunity from liability for any tort except as provided by
5 W.S. 1-39-105 through 1-39-112, 1‑39‑122, and 1-39-123 and
6 35-4-116. Any immunity in actions based on a contract
7 entered into by a governmental entity is waived except to
8 the extent provided by the contract if the contract was
9 within the powers granted to the entity and was properly
10 executed and except as provided in W.S. 1-39-120(b). The
11 claims procedures of W.S. 1-39-113 apply to contractual
12 claims against governmental entities.
13
14 1-39-118. Maximum liability; insurance authorized.
15
16 (d) Except as provided by W.S. 35-4-116, no judgment
17 against a governmental entity shall include an award for
18 exemplary or punitive damages, for interest prior to
19 judgments or for attorney's fees.
20
21 1-42-202. Local government self-insurance program;
22 creation; authorized payments.
23
4 HB0114
2025 STATE OF WYOMING 25LSO-0393
1 (f) Notwithstanding any other provision of this act
2 and except as provided by W.S. 35-4-116, no expenditure
3 shall be made from the program in any action to pay any
4 claim or final judgment for exemplary or punitive damages.
5
6 Section 3. This act is effective immediately upon
7 completion of all acts necessary for a bill to become law
8 as provided by Article 4, Section 8 of the Wyoming
9 Constitution.
10
11 (END)
