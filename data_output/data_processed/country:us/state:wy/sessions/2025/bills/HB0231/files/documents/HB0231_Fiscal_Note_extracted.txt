Medical education funding. 25LSO-0624, 1.1
FISCAL NOTE
University of Wyoming. This appropriation is effective immediately.
DETAIL OF APPROPRIATION
Agency #: 067 Agency Name: University of Wyoming
Unit: N/A
EXPENDITURE BY SERIES AND YEAR FY 2025 FY 2026 FY 2027
0600 Grant & Aid Payments $13,081 $550,808 $550,808
Total Expenditure Per Year: $13,081 $550,808 $550,808
Grand Total Expenditure: $1,114,697
Total Appropriated to Agency: $2,176,313
Total Appropriated by Fund:
GENERAL FUND $2,176,313
Description of appropriation:
The appropriation would provide for five students to attend the University of Utah's
School of Medicine for a four-year program beginning academic year 2025-2026. The
expenditures in the above table reflect the estimated amounts for the first two
years of the students attending medical school based on costs associated with
tuition, fees, and administrative costs. The expenditures show in FY 2025 reflect
the initial administrative costs anticipated by the University of Wyoming. The
remaining appropriation would be spent in fiscal year (FY) 2028 and FY 2029. Any
unexpended, unobligated funds from the appropriation would be reverted by the
University of Wyoming to the General Fund on or before June 30, 2029. The University
of Wyoming would include an amount equal to two times this appropriation in its
standard budget request for the FY 2027-2028 biennium to continue this program for
the next cohort of students.
