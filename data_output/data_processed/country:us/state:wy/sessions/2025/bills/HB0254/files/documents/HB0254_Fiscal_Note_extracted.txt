Section 1:
FISCAL NOTE
This bill contains an appropriation of $93,000 from the GENERAL FUND to the
Legislative Service Office. This appropriation is effective immediately.
DETAIL OF APPROPRIATION
Agency #: 201 Agency Name: Legislative Service Office
Unit: 0101 Administration
EXPENDITURE BY SERIES AND YEAR FY 2025 FY 2026 FY 2027
0900 Contractual Services Costs $0 $93,000 $0
Total Expenditure Per Year: $0 $93,000 $0
Grand Total Expenditure: $93,000
Total Appropriated to Agency: $93,000
Total Appropriated by Fund:
GENERAL FUND $93,000
Description of appropriation:
The appropriation will be expended for the installation of microphones at the
desks of the legislative members of the Wyoming House of Representatives.
The appropriation will be expended for the cost of design, hardware, installation
and programming of the microphones.
It is assumed this appropriation will be expended from the 0900 series
(Contractual Services Costs) in FY 2026.
Prepared by: Dean Temte, LSO Phone: 777-7881
