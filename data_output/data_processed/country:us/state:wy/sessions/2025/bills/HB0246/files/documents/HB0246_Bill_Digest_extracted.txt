"8-4-119. Meat Grinder Monday.
(a) In celebration of the state of Wyoming and in an effort to bring
unity to the state's communities, every Monday shall be known as "Meat Grinder
Monday". Wyoming citizens may wear brown and gold on Monday in recognition of
Meat Grinder Monday.
(b) The governor, not later than July 10, 2025, and not more than once
each year thereafter in advance of a Meat Grinder Monday, shall issue a
proclamation requesting proper observance of "Meat Grinder Monday" as outlined
in subsection (a) of this section.
(c) This section shall not affect commercial paper, the making or
execution of written agreements or judicial proceedings, or authorize public
schools, businesses or state and local government offices to close.". MCKEOWN
