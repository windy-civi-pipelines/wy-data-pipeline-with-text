Section 1:
FISCAL NOTE
This bill contains an appropriation of $250,000 from the GENERAL FUND to the
Legislative Service Office. This appropriation is effective immediately.
DETAIL OF APPROPRIATION
Agency #: 201 Agency Name: Legislative Service Office
Unit: 0101 Administration
EXPENDITURE BY SERIES AND YEAR FY 2025 FY 2026 FY 2027
0200 Supportive Services Costs $50,000 $0 $0
0900 Contractual Services Costs $200,000 $0 $0
Total Expenditure Per Year: $250,000 $0 $0
Grand Total Expenditure: $250,000
Total Appropriated to Agency: $250,000
Total Appropriated by Fund:
GENERAL FUND $250,000
Description of appropriation:
$200,000 will be used to hire a contractor to develop an Apple or Android
application that is compatible to the existing legislative voting system. This
application will authenticate the user as a legislator for each open vote and
allow them to place their vote electronically within the legislative chambers.
$50,000 will be used to purchase hardware for each legislator to submit
electronic votes.
It is assumed that the proposed electronic voting system will be implemented
based on current House and Senate Rules. It is also assumed that more than one
floor vote cannot be open at a given time.
Prepared by: Dean Temte, LSO Phone: 777-7881
(Information provided by Jamie Schaub, Legislative Service Office, 777-6877)
