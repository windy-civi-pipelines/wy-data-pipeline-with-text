AN ACT relating to elections; requiring a qualified elector to be a bona fide
resident of the state of Wyoming for not less than thirty (30) days before the
date of the election in which they offer to vote; requiring documentation
proving residence to register to vote; prohibiting registration based on
documentation showing noncitizen status; making conforming amendments;
requiring rulemaking; and providing for effective dates.
