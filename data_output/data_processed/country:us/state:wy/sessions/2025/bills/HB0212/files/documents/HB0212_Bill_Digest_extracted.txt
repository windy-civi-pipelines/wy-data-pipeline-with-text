HB0212 Local sales and use tax amendments.
Riggins and Strock and Senator(s) Ide and Scott
AN ACT relating to taxation and revenue; increasing the number of local entities
required to approve provisions related to specified local sales and use taxes;
decreasing the amount of time between elections for specified local sales and
use taxes; repealing provisions related to the optional municipal sales and use
tax; specifying applicability; and providing for an effective date.
