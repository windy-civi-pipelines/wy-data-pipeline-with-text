Section 1:
FISCAL NOTE
FY 2026 FY 2027 FY 2028
NON-ADMINISTRATIVE IMPACT
Anticipated Revenue increase
SPECIAL REVENUE FUND $0 $532,000 $532,000
Source of revenue increase:
Universal Service Fund (USF) charge on the retail sales of prepaid wireless
communication access, beginning July 1, 2026 (FY 2027).
Assumptions:
According to the Public Service Commission, the USF rate would be 1.90 percent,
upon the sales of prepaid wireless communication access.
The Department of Revenue already administers a 911 emergency tax of 1.5 percent
on the same sales of prepaid wireless communication access per W.S. 16-9-109.
This tax is primarily collected and reported by retail outlets and convenience
stores. Based on FY 2024 sales of approximately $28.0 million, the revenue
increase to the Universal Service Fund is estimated at $532,000 per year ($28.0
million in Wyoming prepaid wireless sales x 1.90 percent = $532,000).
This bill also extends the sunset date of the Wyoming Telecommunications Act to
July 1, 2031. Under current law, The Wyoming Telecommunications Act is repealed
July 1, 2025.
Prepared by: Dean Temte, LSO Phone: 777-7881
(Information provided by Bret Fanning, Department of Revenue, 777-5200; John
Burbridge, Public Service Commission, 777-7429)
