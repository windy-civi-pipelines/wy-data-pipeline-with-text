FISCAL NOTE
This bill creates the Wyoming Veteran Loan Repayment Program (program) to be
administered by the University of Wyoming (University) and shall expire June 30,

Section 2:
2035.
This bill funds the program by amending the University's budget within the 2024
Budget Bill (2024 Wyoming Session Laws, Chapter 185, Section 2, Section 067) and
adds a footnote to require the University to use $250,000 from the State Aid program
appropriations for the program.
Subject to availability of funds appropriated for this program, loans may be granted
to qualified applicants to pay the cost of attendance for course work leading to a
masters degree in social work or counseling or a doctorate degree in psychology at
the University. The University estimates the current cost of attendance for these
programs at $26,732 for residents and $40,532 for non-residents.
A recipient of a loan under this program may repay the loan without monetary payment
by working not less than 25 percent of the recipient's working hours providing
mental health services in the state to veterans. To qualify for repayment under
this program, work shall be performed within the minimum amount of time necessary
to repay the loan, plus three (3) years, beginning the calendar month in which the
recipient completed the academic degree program.
Repayment of the loan is required for a recipient that fails to complete the
academic program and obtain employment that provides mental health services within
Wyoming within 240 days after completing the academic program.
This bill creates an indeterminable administrative impact on the University due to
the unknown number of students that may participate in the program. Over the past
five years, the University estimates that approximately 13 students may have been
eligible for this program.
