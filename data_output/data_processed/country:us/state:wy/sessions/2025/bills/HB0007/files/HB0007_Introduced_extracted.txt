Section 1:
2025 STATE OF WYOMING 25LSO-0183
K-12 post secondary education options.
Sponsored by: Representative(s) Harshman and Clouston and
Senator(s) Olsen and Schuler
A BILL
for
1 AN ACT relating to education; modifying duties of school
2 district boards of trustees to require access to a minimum
3 of twelve (12) post secondary credit hours for high school
4 students as specified; and providing for an effective date.
5
6 Be It Enacted by the Legislature of the State of Wyoming:
7
8 Section 1. W.S. 21-20-201(h) is amended to read:
9
10 21-20-201. Agreement between districts and
11 postsecondary education institutions required; student
12 participation; credits; financial arrangements;
13 transportation; accessibility; required annual reporting.
14
1 HB0007
2025 STATE OF WYOMING 25LSO-0183
1 (h) Each school district board of trustees shall in
2 conjunction with the University of Wyoming, community
3 college district boards of trustees or other post secondary
4 education institutions accredited by recognized and
5 accepted accrediting agencies, make post secondary
6 education options programs pursuant to this section
7 reasonably accessible to eligible students, which shall
8 include ensuring access to not less than twelve (12) post
9 secondary credit hours to be completed in grades nine (9)
10 through twelve (12).
11
12 Section 2. This act is effective July 1, 2025.
13
14 (END)
