Impact assistance payments to counties are paid out of the General Fund share of
sales and use taxes, up to a specified maximum percentage of the total estimated
material cost of an industrial project in the county.
This bill allows the Industrial Siting Council (Council) to increase the impact
assistance payment maximum allowable percentage to not more than 2.76 percent for
industrial facilities with total estimated materials costs exceeding $350
million. The Council may increase the impact assistance payment percentage if it
finds that the maximum allowable percentage is insufficient to mitigate impacts
of that project. The Council shall forward the proposed increased percentage to
the State Loan and Investment Board for consideration.
While the fiscal impact is indeterminable due to the unknown number and scope of
future industrial projects, estimates of potential fiscal impact at two example
total estimated materials costs are provided below:
• On an approved industrial project with total estimated material costs of
$850 million, the increase in impact assistance payments from the General
Fund could be as much as $6.46 million, if the Council uses the discretion
given in the bill to raise the maximum allowable percentage from 2.0 percent
to 2.76 percent.
• On an approved industrial project with total estimated material costs of
$1.0 billion, the increase in impact assistance payments from the General
Fund could be as much as $12.6 million, if the Council uses the discretion
given in the bill to raise the maximum allowable percentage from 1.5 percent
to 2.76 percent.
This bill shall only apply to impact assistance payments for industrial facility
permit applications submitted on and after the bill’s effective date.
