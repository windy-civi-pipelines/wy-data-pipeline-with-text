Section 2:
Section 1. W.S. 16-9-112 is created to read:
16-9-112. 911 outage reporting.
Any service supplier who owns, operates or controls any
communications equipment necessary for the operation of a
911 emergency reporting system shall, if the failure of the
communications equipment disables, impairs or degrades the
operation of a 911 emergency system to a degree that
materially affects emergency communications through a 911
system, report interruptions of service as required by
rules promulgated by the public service commission.
