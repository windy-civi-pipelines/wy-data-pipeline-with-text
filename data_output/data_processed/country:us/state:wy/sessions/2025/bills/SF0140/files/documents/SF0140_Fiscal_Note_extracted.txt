Trapping education requirements. 25LSO-0227, 1.1
FISCAL NOTE

NON-ADMINISTRATIVE IMPACT
Anticipated Expenditure increase
GAME & FISH FUND $34,894 $25,142 $25,142
Source of expenditure increase:

certificate of trapper education program required by this bill.
Assumptions:
The Department assumes personnel will be required to develop, implement, monitor,
and provide ongoing customer service related to the trapper education program,
estimated at 520 hours per year at the rate of $48.35 per hour.
The Department also assumes programming changes to the licensing system will be
required to implement the requirements of this bill to ensure the certificate of
trapper education can be applied by license type for each sports person, estimated
at 160 hours at the rate of $60.95 per hour for a senior programmer.
The expenditure increase reflected above could be considered an administrative
cost. However, for simplicity and to follow consistent practice on legislation of
this type, it is included on the fiscal note.
