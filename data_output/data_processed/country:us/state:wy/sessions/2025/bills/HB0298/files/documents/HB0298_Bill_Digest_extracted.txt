Section 1:
HB0298 Centralized electronic notice system.
Hutchings
AN ACT relating to the administration of government; requiring the secretary of
state to establish a centralized electronic notice system; requiring
preservation of notices as specified; providing for governmental entities and
other persons to publish public or legal notices on the centralized electronic
notice system in lieu of publication in a newspaper as specified; authorizing
fees; providing definitions; making conforming amendments; requiring
rulemaking; providing an appropriation; and providing for effective dates.
